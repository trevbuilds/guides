Tool 01 · The Mirror Test

For any requirement

Hold it up to the last requirement you wrote. Tick what is true. Fail one, and it only looks like a requirement.

Tick the ones that hold.

Tool 02 · Three lines that hold the line

For any meeting

Calm, not confrontational. Each one puts the decision back with the person who owns it. Copy the one you need.

When the trade-offs are fuzzy and someone wants you to just start writing

"I can document the requirements. First I need the decision rules: what do we optimise for when trade-offs appear?"

When nobody owns the benefit

"If we can't name an owner for the benefit, I'll write 'unassigned' and flag it as a risk."

When scope starts drifting

"I'm not blocking this. I'm asking us to re-open the decision it sits inside."

Tool 03 · The Score

You, and your practice

Eight questions. Four about you, four about the practice you work in. Scored separately on purpose: a strong BA in a weak practice needs a different move from a weak BA in a strong one.

Pick the answer that is true today, not the one you'd like to be true. Each answer describes a concrete picture.

Advisory On-Tap

Every stalled program has the same origin story: a decision that got deferred, escalated, or made by the wrong person. Advisory On-Tap puts a senior decision partner in your corner — on subscription — so the hard calls get made while they're still cheap.

$2,950per month · ex GST · month-to-month
Start on the 1st
What's included every month

Decision sessions

  • Two 60-minute sessions per month, on your calendar rhythm
  • Structured around the decisions in front of you — not a status meeting

Unlimited async counsel

  • Send anything — a vendor proposal, a steering paper, a hard email — and get a considered response within 24 hours
  • No question metering. Ask early, ask often.

Independent review

  • Vendor proposals, SOWs, business cases and quotes reviewed before you sign
  • A second opinion with no stake in the outcome — we don't resell anything

Decision log

  • Every significant call recorded with options, trade-offs and rationale
  • Yours to keep — an audit trail that outlives the subscription
Who it's for

Transformation sponsors, program directors and COOs running complex, multi-vendor programs — who have plenty of delivery capacity but no independent voice at the decision layer.

Executive outcome

Decisions arrive before the cost does. You stop paying the compounding price of indecision, and you build a defensible record of why each call was made.

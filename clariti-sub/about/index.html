About the founder

Trevin Nimaladasa

Over a decade inside transformation programs — as business analyst, transformation lead and program director — across utilities, government, finance and mid-market. ERP, CRM, ITSM, PPM, integration. Oracle, SAP, Business Central, Workday: vendor-agnostic, because the principles are the same.

The pattern behind every stalled program was never delivery capability. It was decision clarity upstream. Clariti was built to fix exactly that — and the subscription model applies the same first-principles thinking to consulting itself: strip the billing complexity, keep the expertise.

Principles
  • Decisions before delivery. The hard calls come first, or delivery executes the wrong priorities well.
  • Structure enables speed. Governance done right removes friction; it doesn't add it.
  • Systems must serve outcomes. ERP, CRM and PPM encode decisions — unclear decisions get automated as confusion.
  • Practical beats theoretical. Every engagement leaves artefacts your team can run without us.
Proof

Selected outcomes.

Tier-1 water utility: multi-vendor PPM program brought under control — data migration readiness, decision traceability, and 310+ requirements produced in four weeks with non-BA SMEs using an AI-assisted method.
Dairy Australia: 40% reduction in manual reporting effort through ERP and process redesign.
Transurban: Workday governance uplift across an HRIS transformation.
£1B UK utility merger: critical integration stabilised to become the most reliable application in the client's landscape.

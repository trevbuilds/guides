Straight answers

Frequently asked.

Is there really no lock-in contract?

Really. Every plan runs month-to-month with 30 days' written notice. If the value isn't obvious each month, you shouldn't be paying for it — that pressure keeps us sharp.

How quickly can the Transformation Office start?

From the 1st of the next month, like every plan. A clarity call first confirms the fit is real — on both sides — then the Office embeds from day one.

How is this different from a contractor or consultancy?

A contractor sells you days; a consultancy sells you a project. A subscription sells you a capability that compounds — the same advisor, accumulating context on your program, month after month, at a fraction of an executive hire.

What if I need more in a given month?

Upgrade for a single month and drop back, or we scope the extra as a fixed-price add-on agreed before it starts. No hourly billing, no surprise invoices.

Which plan should I start with?

Take the free scorecard. If your bottleneck is decision-making, Advisory On-Tap. If governance and traceability are drifting, Program Assurance. If the whole portfolio needs senior hands, apply for the Transformation Office.
